Understanding people, environments and behaviour through visual intelligence.

Nicola Conci is Associate Professor at the University of Trento, where he works in the Multimedia Signal Processing and Understanding Lab (MM-Lab). His research focuses on Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, Human Behaviour Analysis, Digital Twins, Crowd Understanding, 3D Reconstruction, Extended Reality and Multimedia Signal Processing. He has authored more than 150 scientific publications and has coordinated and participated in several national and European research projects.
